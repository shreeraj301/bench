# Indices Methodology
## HCI Programs Research & Comparison Platform

All indices are relative across the six programs in scope and intended as decision aids, not absolute measures. Scores should be accompanied by short rationales and source notes on program pages.

### Livability (1–10)
Measures city-level quality of life for an international graduate student.
- Cost of living and housing availability
- Safety
- International student community and support
- General urban amenities and climate
- Transit and walkability where relevant

### Job Security (1–10)
Measures strength of placement into product design, UX research, and product management roles.
- Historical placement into target roles at competitive companies
- Strength and density of alumni network
- Internship conversion rates
- Program brand recognition in industry
- Location leverage for job search

### Future Proof (1–10)
Measures alignment with the evolving nature of product design and HCI in an AI-heavy world.
- Explicit coverage of human-AI interaction and AI product design
- Systems thinking and strategic product skills
- Balance of research, design craft, and technical fluency
- Curriculum adaptability and elective flexibility
- Ethical and responsible technology emphasis

### Selectivity (1–10)
Relative measure of admissions difficulty.
- Published or estimated acceptance rates
- Typical admitted profile (experience, portfolio strength, academic background)
- Holistic review intensity
- Cohort size relative to applicant volume

### ROI (1–10)
Measures value relative to total cost.
- Total estimated cost of attendance (tuition + living)
- Strength of job outcomes and typical starting roles
- Time to employment
- Funding availability
- Long-term career trajectory support

### Visa Chances (Percentage Range)
Realistic estimate of OPT utilization and employer sponsorship likelihood for international students.
- Program STEM designation and OPT eligibility
- Historical international student outcomes
- Concentration of hiring companies that sponsor
- Location (Bay Area, Seattle, etc. vs. others)
- Program brand strength in tech hiring

**Note:** All ranges and scores should cite sources (employment reports, official pages, cost-of-living data, student discussions) and include a last-updated date. Scores are directional and should be updated as new data becomes available.
